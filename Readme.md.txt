# Superstore Sales Analysis

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes 9,994 Superstore orders to identify sales, profitability, regional, state, product, and discount trends.

An interactive two-page Power BI dashboard was created to present key business insights.

2. Tools Used

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX

3. Key Metrics

- Total Sales: $2.30M
- Total Profit: $286.40K
- Total Quantity: 38K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Average Order Value: $458.61

## Dashboard Pages

 1. Sales Performance Overview
- Sales and profit KPIs
- Sales by category
- Sales by state
- Sales trend by year
- Profit by region
- Top 10 products
- Bottom 5 products
- Interactive filters

 2. State & Profitability Analysis
- Average Order Value
- Top 5 states by sales
- Bottom 5 states by sales
- Top 10 states by profit
- Business insights

## Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
- West region had the highest sales and profit.
- Furniture had high sales but relatively low profit.
- Higher discounts were associated with negative profit.
- Sales increased steadily and were highest in 2017.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product.
- Overall profit margin was 12.47%.
